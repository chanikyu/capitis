Capitis

Capitis is a desktop app for the Mac. Write and browse your own wiki (second brain), and use code editing, a terminal, notebooks and a mailbox (read, summarize and send mail) in one window.

When you connect a Google account in Mail, Capitis reads your mail directly on your Mac and sends mail only when you choose to. Mail is never sent to the Operator's servers. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
